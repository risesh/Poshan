A daily spoon, not a box of supplements

Why we began

Poषण Life foods started because of our own struggles with health caused by the daily stressors in an increasingly fast paced and a life away from nature. 
Over time, we discovered that gentle care and nutrition can solve a lot of problems that seem insurmountable when you're running from one doctor to the next.
We felt that there was a need for products that allow users to live life without worrying about the quality and nutritiousness of their foods.

How we make

We make every product in small batches and often even make some products only when they're ordered, so that you get the freshest and best experience.

There are NO preservatives, fillers or stabilizers in our products and our products are meant to be packed, shipped and consumed fresh. 

WHAT WE BELIEVE
01

Foods Should Heal

What you eat doesn't just fill your stomach, it also determines your mental and physical health

02

Clean and Nutritious
